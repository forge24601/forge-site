Make-up Sessions

Missed a session? Each make-up video walks through the whole session, so you can catch up before the next one.

How to Use a Make-up Session

Watch It, Do the Work, Come Back Ready

There is one make-up video for each session. It teaches what the session taught, in the Facilitator Guide's own words, from the check-in to the homework. Most run 10 to 19 minutes, plus the time you spend on the work.

1. Have your things ready

Your journal, a pen, and your workbook. Some sessions also need your thinking reports or your emotion tracking log; the video says what to have at the start.

2. Stop at the pause sign

When the gold pause sign comes on the screen, stop the video and do the work: answer the check-in questions, do the exercise, write the closing-round answer. Start the video again when you're done. Breathing and grounding practice is done along with the video.

3. Do the homework

Each video ends with the session's homework and the workbook page that goes with it. Bring that work to the next session.

It doesn't replace the class

A big part of FORGE is taking part in it: speaking in the circle, listening, and hearing how other men see the same thing. The video covers the teaching. It can't cover what happened in the room. So use it to catch up, and come to the next session ready to take part.

Ask a man who was there

If you can, ask someone who was in the session what the group talked about. Exercises the men did in pairs can be done with him, too.

For facilitators

The classroom has no internet, so download the make-up onto the playback device before it is needed and test it with sound. Captions are burned in. Some sessions are heavy (trauma, the anger beneath the anger); those make-ups go at a calm pace and tell a man to come to you if it brings up more than he can carry.

About FORGE

FORGE is a peer-led, evidence-based mentorship program designed to build a safer, more stable dorm culture by training men to lead through service, accountability, and conflict reduction.

"To turn time served into time that serves others."
