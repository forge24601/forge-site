Session Media

Videos and facilitator inserts for each FORGE session — watch them here, or download them for class

For Facilitators

Download Before Class

The classroom has no internet, so download each session's videos and its facilitator insert ahead of time and test them on the playback device.

Short videos

Each session has one or two videos of about two to five minutes. They get the session started or set up a segment; they do not replace the facilitator. Captions are burned in, so they work with the sound low.

A facilitator insert

A one-page plan for the full two hours that keeps every segment of the Facilitator Guide, shows where each video plays, and adds discussion questions.

A suggested talk

Most sessions add a short outside talk. Those belong to TED and other publishers and are not hosted here: each one links to YouTube. The insert explains how to get a copy you can play in class.

About FORGE

FORGE is a peer-led, evidence-based mentorship program designed to build a safer, more stable dorm culture by training men to lead through service, accountability, and conflict reduction.

"To turn time served into time that serves others."
