Certificates

Personalize, print and ship FORGE certificates.

Start Here

These are Canva templates

FORGE certificates are Canva templates. Canva is a free online design tool that works in a web browser or the Canva app, and a free account is all you need. Opening a template link gives you your own copy to edit. The original template never changes.

How To

Step by step

  1. Open the template. Click Open in Canva for the certificate you need. Sign in or create a free account, then choose Use template for new design. A copy is saved in your own Canva account.

  2. Add the person’s details. Click the sample name and type the person’s full name, spelled the way they want it. Change the “Awarded” date and the certificate number. Leave the rest of the wording as it is.

  3. Number each certificate. Use the template’s format: the two-letter code, the year, then a three-digit number, for example FM-2026-014. Keep a simple list of names and numbers so no number is used twice.

  4. Doing a whole class? Duplicate the page once per person, then change the name and number on each page. Each page prints as its own certificate.

  5. Check every name. Read each page carefully before ordering. A mistake means a reprint and another wait.

  6. Download the print file. Click Share, then Download, choose PDF Print, and download it. If Canva asks you to pay for a Pro element, that is a one-time charge, and no subscription is needed.

  7. Order printing and shipping. Canva’s own print shop has no certificate product, and its closest Letter-size option comes in batches of 50 per page. So use a print shop that prints your own file, starts at one copy, and ships. FedEx Office certificate printing does all three, and any print shop that prints on cardstock will also work. When you order, choose:

    • Size: 8.5 × 11 in, landscape
    • Paper: heavy cardstock with a matte finish (signatures smear on gloss)
    • Copies: single-sided, one copy of each page
    • Delivery: shipping, not pickup

  8. Ship it to the facility. Use these shipping details:

    • Name: the staff member who coordinates the FORGE class, and their title
    • Company: the facility’s name
    • Address: the facility’s address for packages

    Address the package to that staff member, never to an incarcerated person. Check with them first whether packages need approval and which address to use. Order at least two to three weeks before the ceremony, to allow time for the facility’s mailroom.

  9. Sign them. Certificates arrive with blank signature lines. Sign each one by hand in dark ink before it is presented.

About FORGE

FORGE is a peer-led, evidence-based mentorship program designed to build a safer, more stable dorm culture by training men to lead through service, accountability, and conflict reduction.

"To turn time served into time that serves others."
