Dorm Classes

Shorter classes for every man in a FORGE dorm, taught by FORGE mentors

How It Fits

One Mentor Class. Many Dorm Classes.

FORGE is the class that builds mentors. Not every man in a FORGE dorm is in FORGE, so the dorm runs shorter classes for everyone who lives there, and FORGE mentors teach them. The men get a class built for them; the mentors get practice leading a room.

FORGE

The mentor-building class

  • 36 weeks, three phases, three 2-hour sessions a week
  • For the men who will become certified peer mentors
  • Ends in certification as a FORGE Mentor

How FORGE works →

Dorm Classes

For every man in the dorm

  • One session a week, for a set number of weeks
  • For the men living in the dorm, in FORGE or not; one of them, Introduction to FORGE, is required for every man
  • Taught by FORGE mentors, with a staff sponsor
  • A scripted facilitator guide and a book each man keeps, free to print

The Classes

Ready to Teach

Every class comes with what a mentor needs to run it the first time: a facilitator guide with what to say and when, a book for each man, and a certificate.

Required for every man

Introduction to FORGE

The class every man who moves into a FORGE dorm takes. Not a mentor class: knowing yourself, being a man of your word, having a purpose, and one question at the end of every session, What is your legacy? Join any week; begin within three months of moving in, finish within six.

  • 12 sessions
  • 75–90 minutes each
  • Open entry
  • Live make-up days
About the class and its documents → Ready to teach

Youth Roundtable

Built for the younger men in the dorm, open to anyone. Eight Roundtables on value, finding your voice, respect, the future, family ties and leading without a title. In between, the group plans and runs a Game Day and tournaments for the whole dorm.

  • 8 Roundtables
  • 1 hour each
  • 10 to 15 men
  • Two FORGE mentors lead

About FORGE

FORGE is a peer-led, evidence-based mentorship program designed to build a safer, more stable dorm culture by training men to lead through service, accountability, and conflict reduction.

"To turn time served into time that serves others."
